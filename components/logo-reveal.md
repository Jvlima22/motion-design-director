\# Logo Reveal



\## Purpose



Transform brand identity into a meaningful motion event that introduces,

resolves or connects the product story.



A logo reveal is not:



LOGO

\+

GLOW

\+

SCALE

\+

WHOOSH.



A strong logo reveal communicates:



BRAND CHARACTER



PRODUCT RELATIONSHIP



RECOGNITION



RESOLUTION



The logo should feel like the inevitable visual conclusion of the sequence.



\---



\# 1. When to Use



Use Logo Reveal for:



\- product film opening

\- product film closing

\- SaaS launch

\- app launch

\- brand introduction

\- product-to-brand transition

\- campaign identity

\- feature launch

\- social video

\- end card

\- CTA sequence



\---



\# 2. Logo Roles



A logo can function as:



INTRODUCTION



SIGNATURE



TRANSITION



ORIGIN



DESTINATION



RESOLUTION



CTA ANCHOR



Choose the role before designing motion.



\---



\# 3. Opening Logo



Opening logo should establish:



IDENTITY



TONE



MOTION LANGUAGE



It should not consume excessive time before product appears.



\---



\# 4. Closing Logo



Closing logo should feel like:



RESOLUTION

ANOTHER NEW SCENE.

\# 137. Creative Director Rule



Do not say:



"Make the logo appear with particles and glow."



Define:



SOURCE:

the product's automation workflow completes.



TRANSITION:

active nodes move toward the center while their connection lines shorten.



CONSTRUCTION:

the remaining node geometry maps onto the primary shapes of the brand mark.



RECOGNITION:

once the logo becomes readable, connection lines disappear and all movement

settles.



LIGHT:

only a restrained brand-colored edge remains.



SOUND:

workflow texture resolves into one short tonal lock.



HOLD:

logo remains clean and stable.



CTA:

appears only after brand recognition.



Now the logo feels like the conclusion of the product story.



\---



\# 138. Golden Rule



The logo reveal should not feel attached to the video.



It should feel generated by the video.



PRODUCT

→ IDEA

→ MOTION

→ BRAND.



Use the visual language already established.



Build recognition.



Resolve cleanly.



Then stop moving.
