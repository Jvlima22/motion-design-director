# Social Video

## Role

Specialization for social-first product motion and social adaptations.

Load after:

workflows/product-video-master.md

May be combined with another specialization.

Examples:

APP LAUNCH
+
SOCIAL VIDEO

FEATURE LAUNCH
+
SOCIAL VIDEO

---

# Social Context

Assume attention is fragile.

Do not assume sound is enabled.

Do not assume full-screen viewing.

Do not assume the viewer starts with product context.

---

# Opening

The opening must create immediate orientation, curiosity or value.

Possible opening functions:

- result
- problem
- transformation
- striking product behavior
- visual curiosity
- direct promise
- product reveal

Do not use empty motion merely to create a hook.

---

# First-Frame Test

Ask:

IF THE VIEWER SEES ONLY THE FIRST MOMENT, IS THERE A REASON TO CONTINUE?

A logo alone may not provide one.

---

# Vertical Composition

For 9:16:

RECOMPOSE.

Do not merely crop 16:9.

Reconsider:

- hero scale
- UI crop
- text width
- hierarchy
- camera path
- device orientation
- negative space
- safe zones
- captions
- CTA placement

---

# Silent Comprehension

When sound may be disabled, the core message must survive visually.

This does not mean filling the screen with subtitles.

Use:

- visual causality
- concise copy
- UI state changes
- hierarchy
- product behavior

Audio can strengthen the experience without becoming the only carrier of meaning.

---

# Pacing

Fast does not mean incomprehensible.

Protect:

- product recognition
- important interactions
- reading time
- result comprehension

Compress low-value transitions before compressing important evidence.

---

# Adaptation

When adapting an existing master film:

DO NOT:
crop and export.

Instead:

IDENTIFY CORE MESSAGE
-> IDENTIFY ESSENTIAL SHOTS
-> RECOMPOSE
-> RETIME
-> REWRITE COPY IF REQUIRED
-> REVALIDATE

A social cut is a new communication artifact.

---

# Looping

If looping is required, consider the relationship between:

FINAL STATE
and
OPENING STATE.

Do not force a seamless loop when it damages the ending.

---

# Social Quality Gate

Before finalizing, ask:

DOES THE OPENING EARN ATTENTION?

IS THE PRODUCT RECOGNIZABLE EARLY ENOUGH?

DOES THE CORE MESSAGE WORK WITHOUT SOUND?

IS TEXT LEGIBLE AT MOBILE SIZE?

IS THE PRODUCT EVIDENCE ON SCREEN LONG ENOUGH TO UNDERSTAND?

WAS THE FORMAT RECOMPOSED RATHER THAN CROPPED?

If not, revise before polishing.
